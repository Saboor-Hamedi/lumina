Here are the best, most useful features we can add to the **Table Header**:

### 1. **Table Title & Metadata**
- **Editable Table Title / Caption:** Double-click or click to add a title (e.g. *"Q3 Roadmap"*, *"Sprint Tasks"*).
- **Dimension Badge:** A subtle pill showing `Rows × Columns` (e.g., `4 × 3` or `12 rows`).

---

### 2. **Quick Actions & Export**
- **Copy Table:** One-click copy as **Markdown**, **CSV**, or **JSON**.
- **Export to CSV:** Instantly save or download the table as a `.csv` spreadsheet file.
- **Duplicate Table:** Clone the table directly below.

---

### 3. **Smart Data Tools**
- **Quick Sort:** Sort rows ascending/descending (`A → Z` or `1 → 9`) by clicking a column header.
- **Search & Filter:** A mini inline filter to quickly search through large tables.
- **Column Alignments:** One-click alignment presets (Align Left, Center, Right).

---

### 4. **Display & Structure**
- **Add Row / Column Pills:** Quick `+ Row` and `+ Col` buttons right on the header.
- **Collapse / Expand:** Minimize large tables into a compact summary bar.
- **Wrap vs. Clip Cells:** Toggle between wrapping long cell text or single-line ellipsis.
