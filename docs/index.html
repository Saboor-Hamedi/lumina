Local-First Markdown Notes

Your vault stays on your own disk in plain Markdown. Instant wikilinks, Mermaid diagrams, rich bookmark link cards, and zero vendor lock-in.

Infinite Spatial Canvas

Freehand pencil & highlighter drawing, 34 drag-and-drop diagram shapes, smart auto-routing connectors, frameless titles, and native PNG/SVG export.

Lumina AI Companion

Context-aware assistant supporting DeepSeek, OpenAI, Claude, and local Ollama models with workspace `@mentions`, auto-memory, and live web, arXiv & GitHub search.

2D & 3D Knowledge Graph

Explore connections across notes, tags, and semantic links in real-time 2D and WebGL 3D force-directed graphs.

Native Speed & Privacy

Built with Rust and Tauri v2 for sub-200 MB memory footprint, full touch support, and optional local-only Google Drive backup & Gmail integration.

Cross-Platform Desktop

Consistent experience across Windows, macOS, and Linux with multi-pane split tabs, native PDF viewer, and customizable themes.
