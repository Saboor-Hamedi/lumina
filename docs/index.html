Freehand pencil & highlighter drawing, 34 drag-and-drop diagram shapes, smart auto-routing connectors, frameless titles with font scaling, and native PNG/SVG export.

Lumina AI Companion

Works with DeepSeek, OpenAI, Claude, and local Ollama models. Drag notes into the composer as clickable @mentions, render Mermaid in chat, and search live web, arXiv & GitHub.

2D & 3D Knowledge Graph

Visualize relationships across notes, tags, and semantic links in real-time 2D physics or WebGL 3D with instant search, filtering, and PNG/SVG export.

Private by Architecture
