# Zen Mode & the Command Palette

Lumina gives you two simple ways to stay focused and move quickly: **Zen Mode** clears everything out of the way, and the **Command Palette** finds any action for you.

---

## 🧘 Zen Mode

Press `Ctrl + .` when you want nothing on screen but your writing.

What happens when you turn it on:

- The sidebars, tabs, title bar, status bar, and breadcrumbs all disappear at once.
- Only your note and your thoughts remain.
- The change is instant, with no sliding panels or waiting.

When you are ready to come back, press `Ctrl + .` again. Lumina restores everything exactly as it was, including any panels you had open.

If the title bar is hidden, you can still move the window by dragging the very top edge of the app.

**Zen Mode is useful for:**

- Writing a first draft
- Reading or editing closely
- Presenting your work without distractions
- Any moment when the screen feels too busy

---

## ⚡ The Command Palette

Think of the Command Palette as a search box for the whole app. Instead of hunting through menus, you type what you want to do.

Open it with `Ctrl + Shift + P`, or type `>` in the search bar.

**How to use it:**

1. Type a few words, such as "theme", "export", or "terminal".
2. Use the arrow keys to choose a result.
3. Press `Enter` to run it.

You can also change settings right inside the palette:

- **On or off settings** switch with a single press.
- **Choice settings** show a short list of options to pick from.
- **Number settings**, such as font size or window brightness, offer preset values and also accept a number you type yourself.

### Show or hide parts of the app

The palette can tidy your workspace too. Search for the part you want and switch it on or off:

- Title bar
- Status bar
- Activity bar (the row of main icons)
- Tabs
- Breadcrumbs (the path at the top of a note)
- Primary sidebar (`Ctrl + B`)
- Secondary sidebar

---

## 🕸️ The Knowledge Graph

Press `Ctrl + G` to see your notes as a visual map in its own tab.

- Each note appears as a point, and the links between notes connect them.
- Search by title or tag to find a note quickly.
- Click a point to open that note.
- Save the map as a picture or as a scalable image when you want to share it.

---

## 🎨 A Calmer Sidebar

When you are not in Zen Mode, press `Ctrl + B` to slide the main sidebar in or out. The movement is smooth and keeps your note in place, so you never lose your spot in the text.

---

> **Tip:** Start with `Ctrl + .` to clear your screen and `Ctrl + Shift + P` to find anything. Those two shortcuts cover most of what you need.
